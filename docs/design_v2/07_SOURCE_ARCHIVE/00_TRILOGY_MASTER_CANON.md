# Trilogy Master Canon

## Authority and scope

This is the current story canon for the new *Echoes of the Aether* trilogy, drawn from
creator statements in the project conversation and compatible older source documents.
The creator may revise any detail. A direct later creator decision supersedes an
earlier one.

The older project canon remains preserved. Where it describes the former online
co-op-only game, its story structure and ability timing do not govern this trilogy.

## Three-game plan

1. **Echoes of the Aether: Aster** — first game; single-player story starring Aster.
2. **Echoes of the Aether: Orion** — second game; single-player story starring Orion.
   Aster is an NPC ally who accompanies and fights beside Orion.
3. **Echoes of the Aether: The Lost Echoes** — third game; cooperative game featuring
   both Aster and Orion, currently planned as the trilogy finale.

The three games are one continuous saga, not unrelated stories. Aster's story ends with
him trapped behind a warp-broken gate. Orion's game continues this crisis, frees Aster
and grows their friendship. They begin their journey together, which leads into the
co-op finale.

The creator wants to complete Aster's story and game plan first, then begin production.
The working playtime target for Aster's game is about 8–10 hours. The possible one- or
two-chapter Orion game and six-chapter finale are current flexible planning ideas.
The final game may be the end of the trilogy, with future games possible if the series
receives support.

## Main world facts

- The setting is a spacefaring universe powered by Echo-related technology.
- **Eldara** is Aster's planet.
- **Veraya** is Orion's planet.
- **Nalora** is the ancient civilization's planet, at the edge of the universe.
- The planet dungeons on Eldara and Veraya become joined by an Echo Warp. The planets
  and dimensions do not merge.
- **Aether** is the afterlife destination of Echoes, not the name of a planet.

## Main story facts

- Eldara has a guild town and a vast dungeon. Hunters fight monsters, gather valuable
  finds and trade them through guilds.
- Aster is an experienced hunter who enters the dungeon seeking the legendary Echo
  Rewind relic. A legendary adventuring team once reached near it but failed because of
  space-time distortion.
- An Echo Warp joins parts of Eldara's and Veraya's dungeons, strengthens the monsters
  and traps Aster. A map or clue found at the end of Chapter 1 leads toward Echo Rewind.
- Aster obtains Echo Rewind around the middle of his game. It is his only special
  ability.
- Aster reaches the final dungeon and defeats a space-time boss before the final gate.
  The Echo Warp has broken the gate, and Aster cannot open it. He remains trapped.
- Orion's game continues with Orion entering the crisis. He uses engineering, strategy,
  Echo Intel and puzzles to help Aster escape. Aster is an NPC ally and fights beside
  the player-controlled Orion.
- The third game follows their joint expedition to Nalora, the rumored origin of
  legendary relics. They find a broken ship and must solve its puzzles, face monsters,
  uncover the Echoes' history and escape the planet.

## Canon and current flexibility

The creator has said that the ideas developed in chat are intended for the games and
are canon. Flexible proposals—such as exact region layouts, puzzle ordering, boss
names, chapter lengths, AI details and hour allocations—remain current canon while
open to revision. Keep clearly labeled placeholders where a proper name is not
confirmed.
