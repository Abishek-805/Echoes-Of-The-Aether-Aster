# Eldara — world of hunters

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

Temperate forests, broad valleys, open skies, old roads, frontier settlements and an accessible Vaelari sanctuary. Spacefaring society, not medieval magic; local frontier uses practical human engineering and recovered technology. Warm bronze, forest green, worn fabric and readable bolted metal. Guild town is built around accessible sanctuary entrance, not the planet's only city.

## Society
Guild licenses hunters to recover loot, protect routes and report hazards. Copper trainees; Iron established routes; Silver dangerous territories; Gold advanced expeditions (Aster); Vanguard unknown-region elites (Dawn Expedition). Rank is cultural reputation, not grindable XP. Guild town provides work contracts, trade, cartography, repair and welfare. Orphanage is supported by community and hunters; Mira raised Aster there. Release Houses safely release Echoes ceremonially. Relic Commission assesses dangerous artifacts; Cartographer's Office maintains maps; Bram's shop adapts gear. Most hunters stay in profitable outer grounds because deeper paths are unsafe, not because they lack bravery.

## Playable town
A short interactive opening: street and memorial; orphanage; guild and map desk; artificer practice yard; gate. NPCs return from hunts; overheard rumor develops into Riven's partial report. The player may purchase optional supplies with starting Guild Marks and practice heat/EE. Aster leaves alone for a familiar survey, not a knowingly suicidal expedition. The Warp cuts town off after departure; no later merchant quests or return path. See [Chapter 1](../03_ASTER_GAME/CHAPTER_01.md).
