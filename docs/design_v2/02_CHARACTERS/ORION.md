# Orion — character bible

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

Male, 23–27 working age range, talented engineer/strategist from Veraya. Researcher first, inventor and practical repair specialist. Intelligent, curious, precise, calm, compassionate; not arrogant, aggressive or comedic. Believes everything can be understood, but incomplete information and human decisions challenge him. Fears irreversible mistakes. II: applies knowledge under uncertainty, rescues Aster; III: learns ethical responsibility and completes Relay. Never automatic repair.

Lean, agile, lightly athletic, practical researcher silhouette, modular tools, repairable human manufacturing. Right-forearm Intel mount becomes active only on acquisition during II; compact industrial housing, no supernatural motifs. Echo Collector separate from Intel; same safe-release EE principle. Sidearm heat-based, ordinary tool distractor, smoke emitter candidate (legacy reusable cooldown-based support), repair kit, diagnostic terminal, backpack. Orion invented an expedition rifle in earlier material; retain as engineering achievement without implying the heroes already know one another in I.

Intel: physical contact with unfamiliar device, 1–2 s diagnostic interaction, EE cost (prototype 8), returns purpose, fault, prerequisites and safe repair procedure. Player completes engineering manually. Repeat known scan free from cached journal; mandatory scan station supplies energy. Relay: concept only II; validated endpoints, no combat escape, completion quest III. Legacy Relay can support partner travel/summon and return only under validated safe conditions; do not implement in I or II.

NPC partnership: Aster fights while Orion solves hazards; Orion can issue follow/hold/engage/retreat commands. Neither is a disposable escort. Detailed AI and co-op netcode are later production work. [Game II](../06_FUTURE_GAMES/ORION_GAME_DIRECTION.md).
