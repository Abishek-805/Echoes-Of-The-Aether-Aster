# Worlds and Story Canon

## Eldara — Aster's world

- Eldara is Aster's home planet.
- A guild town supports a culture of dungeon hunters. Citizens venture into the
  dungeon, defeat monsters, collect valuable finds and sell loot through guilds.
- The dungeon is vast and varied: it can contain trees, open sky, water, ruins and
  constructed spaces rather than being only a cave.
- Aster's first game opens in town on a normal day. He hears a rumor that the legendary
  Echo Rewind relic may be in the dungeon and enters to find it.
- The dungeon is a connected world-scale puzzle. Its geography, structures, relics,
  creatures and Echo systems all tell a story and give clues Aster needs to understand
  the world and find a way out.

## Veraya — Orion's world

- Veraya is Orion's planet and has its own dungeon.
- An Echo Warp joins parts of its dungeon with Eldara's dungeon.
- The worlds do not merge; the dungeon pathways do.
- Orion's game follows his perspective on the crisis. Aster accompanies him as an NPC
  ally and fights beside him.

## Nalora — ancient planet and finale

- Nalora is at the edge of the universe and is rumored to be the ancient civilization's
  planet and the origin of legendary relics.
- The ancient civilization created powerful relics and distributed them to other
  peoples across the universe. Relics were kept in dungeons as significant keepsakes or
  memorials and protected there.
- Nobody in the heroes' world has seen Nalora; its existence begins as rumor.
- Aster and Orion travel there expecting people and find monsters instead.
- The failed Echo-gathering project and Echo Warp displaced Nalora's people and
  transported aliens there. The aliens now occupy Nalora and burn Echoes to survive.
- Many ancient people were scattered or died; some were killed by aliens. Whether
  survivors or descendants remain is open for possible future stories.
- Aster and Orion find a broken ship, solve its puzzles, face the monsters, gather
  further relics and try to escape the planet. The Echo Relay quest belongs here.

## Story arc of the three games

### 1. *Echoes of the Aether: Aster*

Aster is an orphan who grew up in an orphanage and became an experienced hunter. He
enters Eldara's dungeon seeking the rumored Echo Rewind relic, after a legendary team
failed to reach it through space-time distortion. An Echo Warp joins Eldara's and
Veraya's dungeon pathways and traps Aster. He must understand the dungeon as a whole
and survive its stronger monsters. A map clue leads him toward Echo Rewind, which he
finds around mid-game. After fighting a space-time boss at the final dungeon, Aster
reaches a gate broken by the Warp and cannot open it. He is left trapped.

### 2. *Echoes of the Aether: Orion*

Orion's solo game takes place on Veraya and follows his side of the dungeon crisis.
He enters the dungeon and is trapped by an Echo Warp. The player controls Orion; Aster
is an NPC ally who accompanies and fights with him. Orion uses engineering, strategy,
Echo Intel and puzzle solving to help them both escape through the final gate. Their
friendship grows and they begin their journey together. This game may be one or two
chapters; that scope is flexible.

### 3. *Echoes of the Aether: The Lost Echoes*

After discovering the power and history of legendary relics, Aster and Orion set out
to find rumored Nalora. They locate it at the edge of the universe, discover the
monster-occupied world and a broken ship, and work through puzzles to escape. The
larger mystery reveals the ancient civilization's failed attempt to collect all
universe-wide Echoes, the resulting Warp and the fate of the trapped Echoes. A quest
leads to the discovery or completion of Orion's Echo Relay. Six chapters is the
creator's current flexible idea for this finale.

The finale is currently intended to conclude the trilogy. Future stories may be
possible if the games receive support.
