# Echo Systems Canon

## Echoes and Aether

- An Echo is the unspoken voice or remaining impression of a dead person.
- Aether is the afterlife destination for Echoes; it is not a planet.
- Echoes have metaphysical meaning and are not generic batteries or crystals.
- Equipment can release an Echo and convert the released energy into usable power,
  allowing the Echo to continue to Aether.
- Alien creatures consume or burn Echoes outright to use them as energy. This is a
  destructive process distinct from controlled release.
- In Aster's game, he gathers Echoes from defeated monsters to power his weapons and
  equipment by controlled release.
- More efficient use of Echoes comes from the ancient civilization's advanced
  technology and is learned in the final game.

## Echo Rewind — Aster

- Echo Rewind is Aster's only special ability.
- Aster finds/acquires the relic around the middle of his first game; he does not start
  with it.
- It rewinds Aster's own recent position and personal state. It does not rewind the
  world, enemies or Orion.
- The legacy system describes a compact right-hand device and an approximately
  six-second personal rewind as a tuning target. Retain these as inherited system
  detail unless the creator revises them during single-player design.
- The ability supports combat, traversal and the connected-world puzzle design.

## Echo Intel — Orion

- Orion gains/finds Echo Intel during his solo game.
- It analyzes unfamiliar devices and helps Orion understand their function, condition
  and repair needs.
- Echo Intel provides understanding; Orion still performs the repair and engineering
  work himself.
- The old character source describes a right-forearm analysis device. Its presentation
  may be adapted to the new game while retaining the understanding-not-auto-repair
  principle.

## Echo Warp

- Echo Warp is an uncontrolled space-time distortion, distinct from Echo Relay.
- The ancient civilization attempted to gather Echoes dispersed across the universe
  onto Nalora. The project failed; extreme Echo concentration caused Echo Warp events.
- The phenomenon can occur frequently and randomly after that failure.
- It displaced the ancient people from Nalora across space and transported aliens from
  their home planet to Nalora; many ancient people were lost or killed.
- In the current trilogy story it also joins parts of Eldara's and Veraya's dungeons,
  strengthens their monsters and traps Aster.
- The warp damages the final gate in Aster's game, leaving it impossible for him to
  open. The characters do not control or reproduce the Warp.

## Echo Relay — Orion

- Echo Relay is Orion's concept for advanced space-time teleportation.
- Orion cannot complete it in his solo game because the technology is too advanced.
- Finding or completing Echo Relay is a quest in *Echoes of the Aether: The Lost
  Echoes*, the third game.
- It is not a separate “Echo Teleport” power and is not available to Aster in his
  first game.
- The earlier co-op source contains detailed Relay mechanics. Those mechanics can
  inform the finale after review, but the new trilogy's discovery/completion timing
  takes precedence.
