## Reveal contract
I resolves relic, dungeon function, local Warp effects, gate damage; only fragmented Nalora name and convergence warning. II resolves rescue and local node topology, reconstructs navigation. III explains project politics, displacement, Voruun ecology and lost Echoes; completes Relay. The afterlife's exact nature, Voruun homeworld name and surviving Vaelari descendants are intentional future mysteries. Do not imply the heroes know full history in I.
