# Creature ecology and Echo-dependent life

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

| Class | Origin | Energy and behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Native fauna | Eldara/Veraya | Ordinary food chains, territorial/defensive; small creatures can be non-hostile |
| Echo-adapted fauna | Local organisms | Leaked energy supplements diet; some temporarily bind Echoes |
| Constructs | Vaelari systems | Security/maintenance directives, trapped Echo containment, corrupted by Warp |
| Warp-touched | Altered/displaced creatures | Local spatial anomalies; predictable telegraphs despite unusual movement |
| Voruun | Unnamed displaced alien world | Directly consume/destroy Echoes; no inherent moral evil, survival-driven |

Foragers patrol bound-Echo hotspots; Leech clusters near overloaded conduits; guards patrol old paths; turrets protect archives; Skitters and Stalkers use existing marked folds. Reservoir repair decreases certain local populations; use a persistent world-state variant, not unpredictable random change. Defeated creatures yield previously bound, unconsumed Echoes for release, plus materials. If no bound Echo remains, yield only materials or preexisting residual EE; no creation of dead persons. Voruun feeding is shown explicitly only when appropriate to reveal order. Do not imply killing a nonhuman creature automatically creates a human Echo. Respawns and repeated rewards are gameplay abstractions; no literal resurrection or duplicated individual Echo.

Food web: plants/fungi -> small native grazers -> Foragers -> larger predators; Echo accumulation attracts adapted organisms. Construct maintenance loops are independent. Voruun are rare evidence in I, greater presence in III. The final release reduces the central food source without requiring extermination; long-term adaptation is an intentional sequel hook.
