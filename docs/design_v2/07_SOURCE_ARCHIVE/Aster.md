1. Character Overview
Summary
Aster is one of the two playable characters in Echoes of the Aether. He serves as the expedition's ranger and primary combat specialist, responsible for exploring hostile environments, protecting Orion, recovering ancient technology, and ensuring the team's survival.

His defining gameplay ability is Echo Rewind, allowing him to reverse his own position and state for tactical repositioning without affecting the rest of the world.

3. Design Pillars
Every future design decision should support these pillars.

Pillar 01 — Mobility
Movement is Aster's greatest defense.

Equipment should never appear heavy enough to restrict agility.

Pillar 02 — Human Engineering
Everything Aster carries is manufactured by the expedition.

Visible construction methods include:

Bolts

Fasteners

Mechanical hinges

Replaceable panels

Utility straps

This intentionally contrasts with the seamless design language of ancient Echo technology.

Pillar 03 — Readability
The player should recognize Aster immediately from gameplay scale.

Priority silhouette:

Rifle

Echo Device

Broad shoulders

Backpack

Utility belt

Pillar 04 — Purposeful Equipment
Every visible object has a reason to exist.

No decorative armor.

No unnecessary pouches.

No ornamental details.

Pillar 05 — Echo Rewind
Echo Rewind is Aster's defining mechanic, but it should not dominate his appearance. When inactive, the device blends naturally into his equipment. Only during activation does it become visually prominent through its cyan energy channels and pulse effects.

9. Echo Rewind Specification
Objective: Define Echo Rewind's production behavior while remaining faithful to the GDD. This section expands presentation and implementation details without changing the established gameplay rules.

Ability Summary
Ability Name

Echo Rewind

Character

Aster

Category

Core Ability

Gameplay Purpose

Tactical repositioning and survival.

Core Rules
The GDD defines Echo Rewind as:

Rewinds only Aster.

Reverses Aster's position and personal state.

Does not affect Orion.

Does not rewind enemies.

Does not rewind the world.

These rules are immutable unless the GDD is intentionally revised.

Design Pillars
Every implementation should reinforce these principles.

Personal
The ability belongs to Aster alone.

Players should never mistake it for global time manipulation.

Fast
The rewind should feel immediate.

It is an action used during combat, not a cinematic event.

Readable
Players should instantly understand:

Ability activated

Aster is rewinding

Rewind complete

Ability cooling down

without relying entirely on UI.

Technological
Presentation should communicate Echo technology, not magic.

No mystical runes.

No fantasy effects.

No reality tearing.

Animation Specification
State 01 — Idle
Device:

Very faint pulse.

No constant glow.

Character:

Standard idle animation.

State 02 — Activation
Duration:

Very short.

Key poses:

Right hand opens slightly.

Aster clenches his fist.

Device channels illuminate.

Cyan pulse forms.

This fist-clench is Aster's signature animation.

State 03 — Rewind
Character:

Body retraces its previous movement.

Echo afterimage briefly marks the previous path.

Movement feels smooth and deliberate rather than teleporting.

World:

Unchanged.

Enemies:

Continue acting normally.

State 04 — Recovery
Aster regains his stance immediately.

No exaggerated recovery.

Player control returns as soon as gameplay allows.

VFX Specification

Brief energy bloom.

Character

Faint distortion around the body.

Environment

Audio Direction
Activation

During Rewind
Layered soundscape:

Low-frequency resonance.

Soft crystalline harmonics.

Completion

Gameplay Readability
Without looking at the HUD, players should know:

Rewind in progress.

Rewind finished.

Device unavailable (cooldown).

The device itself should communicate these states through its lighting and subtle animation.

Archive Policy
When a new major version is approved:

Preserve the previous version unchanged.

Archive it in a dedicated Archive/ directory.

Update references to point to the latest approved version.

Record the reason for the supersession.

This ensures historical decisions remain accessible without confusing the current production team.
