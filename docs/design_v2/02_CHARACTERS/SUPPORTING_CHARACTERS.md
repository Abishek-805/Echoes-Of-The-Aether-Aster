Example voices: Mira: “A promise to come home is worth more than a trophy.” Garran: “Survey the known route. If it changes, turn back.” Liora: “A map is a record, not a guarantee.” Bram: “Let the vents breathe. And remember—the light leaving the chamber is not the energy you keep.” Riven: “We could see the vault. Every step took us farther away.” Seren: “A memory preserved against its will is not a memory saved.”

Dawn Expedition: renowned Vanguard team, explored years earlier; failed in a localized distortion, left survey records, not a secret conspiracy. Riven is a confirmed survivor; exact fate of every other member isn't needed to establish the main route. Optional Last Survey resolves their expedition failure without inventing a hidden villain. After Warp Aster is mostly solitary. No living Seren NPC in I. No Orion face/reveal before post-credits beyond anonymous evidence.
