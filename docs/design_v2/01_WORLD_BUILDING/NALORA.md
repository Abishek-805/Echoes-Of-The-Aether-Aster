# Nalora — world of the lost

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

Nalora lies at the edge of the known universe. Rumored origin of legendary relics; no one known to the heroes has visited it before their expedition. Ancient Vaelari cities, memorial plazas, infrastructure and gardens show ordinary lives before disaster. Its visual identity combines pale alloy, violet distortion, muted vegetation, dark Voruun growth and quiet skies. The Great Fracture displaced many inhabitants and brought Voruun; present landscape is monster occupied. Some survivors may exist beyond the planet, never confirmed in the trilogy.

The protagonists' ship is damaged near arrival by a Warp; a separate broken Vaelari vessel becomes the escape goal. Regions in III: silent landing, city of memories, devouring wilds, Convergence research, central release infrastructure, ancient ship. The active preservation system continues collection. The finale uses safe controlled release, independent ship energy and shutdown of the central gathering process, not the destruction of trapped Echoes. Local damaged structures may remain unsafe. Voruun are not exterminated; the survivors retreat toward residual sources and the ecological outcome beyond the central area stays open. The original homeworld of the Voruun remains unnamed.
