# Audio and music bible

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

Music reinforces culture and emotional progression. Eldara warm acoustic/industrial accents; Veraya precise electronic textures; Vaelari solemn spacious motif; Warp introduces fractured fragments rather than constant noise. Aster motif strong rhythmic phrase; Orion motif measured melodic response; finale combines both. The memorial melody returns at Nalora release. Silent Gallery and broken-gate scene use quiet ambience, not melodramatic score.

Audio buses master/music/SFX/UI/voice, normalized consistent levels, adjustable independently. Use subtitles and visual alternatives for every critical cue; stereo panning never sole location indicator. No audio stutter on pause/scene transition; loop crossfades, duck music for dialogue. Record license and attribution for each clip; use original or properly licensed work, not reference-game tracks. Production target six region music loops, town theme, three boss motif variants, finale/credits and ambient layers; reuse responsibly. Audio IDs stable in Godot data. No finished audio included.
