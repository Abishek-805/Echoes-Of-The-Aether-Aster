# Resonance Sanctuary architecture and visual grammar

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

Originally public memorial gardens, archive, research and relic-preservation facilities. Protected inner vaults and fixed transit infrastructure support service workers. Open sky is possible because the complex spans real valleys, gardens and subterranean facilities. Some warped areas belong physically to Veraya; no planets merge. Modern hunters call the ruins dungeons. Ancient network nodes share a resonance signature and can become accidentally connected.

| Symbol | Shape | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Ring with central dot and rays | Supply |
| Conduit | Parallel chevrons | Direction of flow |
| Receiver | Square with inset U | Device powered |
| Archive | Open-book form | Records |
| Anchor | Diamond with axis and central circle | Spatial stability |
| Release | Upward layered triangle and baseline | Safe passage |

Teach on signage in Outer Wilds; reuse at Sluice, Vault, Reservoir, Sanctuary, Final Approach. Shape and audio accompany color. Root Arch/Hanging Bell/Split Stone are local conductor identifiers, not competing universal categories. Eldaran additions: ropes, wood, bolts; Verayan: precision panels; Vaelari: seamless curves, repeated motifs. Warp: misaligned geometry and violet cracks, consistent safe outlines. Every room has original function, present condition, useful landmark and navigational purpose. A repaired mechanism visibly changes connected rooms; no random mandatory topology.
