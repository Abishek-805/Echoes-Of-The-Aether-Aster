# Veraya — world of innovation

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

Research cities, engineering universities, laboratories, precision manufacture and a culture of measurement. Cool steel, pale blue and warm indicator lamps distinguish local adaptations from Eldaran hunter structures. Residents can travel between known worlds, but Nalora's damaged coordinates remain unknown. Neither culture is superior: Eldara excels at fieldcraft; Veraya at diagnosis. Their disagreements are professional stereotypes, not universal traits.

The local Resonance Sanctuary shares Vaelari architecture but has research annexes, diagnostic terminals and modern measurement equipment. The Warp connects only its trapped segments to Eldara. In Game I, Veraya Fold reveals unfamiliar skies, plant life, equipment marks and the anonymous line “The structures aren't moving. The connections between them are changing.” Orion's research authored that recording before the crisis; a separate diagnostic channel later receives Aster's call. In II the player sees Orion's institute, entry, secondary Warp, annex, Intel acquisition and repair path. They escape through a surviving service corridor to Veraya. No direct interplanetary transport ability is introduced in I or II.
