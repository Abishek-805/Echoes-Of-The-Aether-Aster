# The Vaelari and the Great Fracture

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

The Vaelari (ancient people of Nalora) believed knowledge should be shared, contributions remembered, technology should protect life. Their relics traveled among worlds as gifts, memorials and specialist tools. Architecture is seamless, curved, patterned, made of pale alloy and embedded conduits. Their specialist Personal Continuity Device (Echo Rewind) enabled rescue personnel to recover from recent personal hazards; never reversed history. The sanctuaries served public gardens, archives, research, safe release and protected relic custody.

| Faction | Value | Role in disaster | Record voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Custodians | Passage to Aether | Warned against permanent storage | “A memory preserved against its will is not a memory saved.” |
| Continuum | Preserve memories indefinitely | Expanded collection to all Echoes | “No memory should ever be lost.” |
| Wayfarers | Shared infrastructure and exploration | Supplied network nodes, some resisted expansion | “A passage connects more than two places.” |

Seren, ancient researcher, provides partial warnings in I and fuller records in III. Their exact fate is unknown. The Convergence Project started as protection/study of endangered Echoes, then expanded to universe-wide collection. Intake exceeded release, space-time destabilized and the Great Fracture scattered or killed many Vaelari, displaced Voruun to Nalora and stranded vast Echo populations. Some displaced Vaelari were killed by aliens. The network and automated preservation systems continued their directives without adequate oversight. No secret evil mastermind; the tragedy arose from fear of loss and irresponsible escalation.

The surviving network links sanctuary nodes, making them prone to local Warp. Nalora's navigation references fragmented, preventing ordinary expeditions from locating it. The modern worlds know relics but not confirmed coordinates. Game I shows names and incomplete warnings, II triangulates, III explains the project's factions and shuts down central collection. Destroyed Echoes cannot be recovered. Surviving displaced Vaelari and the original Voruun homeworld remain intentionally open.
