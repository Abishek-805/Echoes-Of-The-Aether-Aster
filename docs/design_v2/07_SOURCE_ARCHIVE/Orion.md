1. Character Overview
Summary
Orion is one of the two playable characters in Echoes of the Aether and serves as the expedition's Technology Researcher, Engineer, and Inventor. His expertise lies in understanding, restoring, and developing technology that enables the expedition to progress through hostile and mysterious environments.

Unlike Aster, who protects the expedition through combat, Orion overcomes obstacles through intelligence, engineering, and scientific analysis. His ability to rapidly interpret unfamiliar technology allows the team to restore forgotten machinery, repair recovered artifacts, and unlock paths that would otherwise remain inaccessible.

Orion is also the inventor of the expedition's starting rifle, demonstrating that his talents extend beyond research into practical engineering and innovation.

Character Role
Narrative
Orion represents humanity's pursuit of knowledge.

His primary narrative responsibilities are to:

Research ancient technology.

Restore damaged machinery.

Analyze Echo technology.

Repair expedition equipment.

Where Aster secures the expedition's survival through strength, Orion ensures its progress through discovery and understanding.

Orion's gameplay focuses on enabling progress rather than defeating enemies.

Players should feel that every obstacle has a solution waiting to be discovered.

Core Responsibilities
Throughout the adventure, Orion consistently fulfills the following roles:

Develop new tools and weapons when resources allow.

Interpret Echo technology through Echo Intel.

Enable access to areas that would otherwise remain unreachable.

Support the expedition's long-term survival through knowledge and innovation.

Signature Ability — Echo Intel
Orion's defining gameplay ability is Echo Intel.

By activating the device mounted on his right forearm and making physical contact with a piece of technology, Orion can rapidly analyze and understand it.

Echo Intel allows him to determine:

The technology's purpose.

Its operating principles.

Existing damage or faults.

The requirements for repair or restoration.

Safe methods of activation.

The analysis is nearly instantaneous, presented through a brief 1–2 second interaction animation.

Echo Intel does not repair or activate technology automatically. Instead, it provides Orion with the knowledge required to solve the problem using his own expertise.

Activation of Echo Intel requires Echoes, reinforcing its connection to the game's progression and resource systems.

Engineering and invention.

Restoration of ancient technology.

Limitations

His limitations include:

Lower effectiveness in direct confrontations.

Reliance on Aster during dangerous engagements.

Dependence on available resources to develop and restore technology.

Echo Intel requires Echoes to function.

These limitations encourage cooperation between the two protagonists and reinforce their complementary roles.

Relationship with Aster
Aster and Orion are partners whose strengths compensate for each other's weaknesses.

Aster dedicated his life to becoming strong enough to protect the expedition.

Orion dedicated his life to understanding technology, accepting that he would never reach Aster's level of combat skill.

Their partnership is built on mutual trust:

Aster protects Orion during hostile encounters.

Orion enables Aster to continue the mission by solving technological challenges, restoring equipment, and developing new tools.

Neither character can fulfill the expedition's objectives alone.

5. Gameplay Design & Mechanics
Objective: Define Orion's gameplay systems, player interactions, progression, and unique mechanics. Orion's gameplay should reward intelligence, observation, and engineering rather than direct combat.

Gameplay Philosophy
Orion is designed around one core principle:

"Knowledge unlocks progress."

Unlike Aster, who overcomes challenges through combat and survival, Orion advances the expedition by understanding technology, restoring forgotten systems, and solving complex environmental problems.

Players controlling Orion should constantly feel encouraged to:

Observe before acting.

Analyze before repairing.

Think before fighting.

Experiment with technology.

Core Gameplay Loop
Orion's gameplay follows a repeating cycle:

Explore the environment.

Discover unfamiliar technology.

Analyze it using Echo Intel.

Understand its function and condition.

Gather required resources.

Repair or activate the system.

Unlock new paths, tools, or story progression.

This loop reinforces Orion's identity as the expedition's technological expert.

Primary Ability — Echo Intel
Overview
Echo Intel is Orion's signature gameplay mechanic.

It allows players to interact with technology that is inaccessible to other characters.

Activation
Player Input:

Activate Echo Intel.

Approach compatible technology.

Interact with the object.

Scan Sequence
Orion raises his right-hand Echo Intel device.

Echo energy activates.

Orion places his hand on the target.

A 1–2 second scan animation plays.

Diagnostic information is displayed.

The player receives analysis results.

Information Revealed
Echo Intel can reveal:

Device name.

Primary function.

Operational status.

Damaged components.

Missing parts.

Repair requirements.

Power source.

Safety warnings.

Historical information (when applicable).

7. Personality & Narrative Design
Objective: Define Orion's personality, motivations, relationships, and narrative role. Every interaction should reinforce his identity as a brilliant researcher whose greatest strength is knowledge.

Narrative Purpose
Orion represents humanity's endless curiosity.

Where others see abandoned ruins, Orion sees unanswered questions.

Where others fear ancient technology, Orion sees an opportunity to learn.

His role in the story is to bridge the gap between the present world and the forgotten civilization through understanding rather than conquest.

Core Motivation
Orion's primary motivation is to understand.

He believes that every machine, every invention, and every forgotten structure was created for a reason.

Discovering that reason is what drives him forward.

Knowledge is never pursued for power.

Echo Relay
Ability Classification
Type: Mobility / Exploration / Co-op Support

Status: Story Unlock

Overview
Echo Relay is Orion's signature invention, developed during the expedition after studying Ancient Echo Resonance technology.

Unlike Echo Intel, which allows Orion to understand Ancient technology, Echo Relay is an entirely new invention created by Orion himself.

Its purpose is not combat, but to improve teamwork by eliminating unnecessary backtracking and enabling efficient cooperation between Aster and Orion.

Story Progression
Orion does not possess Echo Relay at the beginning of the game.

Throughout the expedition, he studies additional Ancient technology and eventually develops the first working Echo Relay prototype.

Unlocking Echo Relay represents a major milestone in Orion's growth from researcher to inventor.

Activation Device
Echo Relay operates through a dedicated handheld relay device designed and built by Orion.

This device is separate from the Echo Intel device and serves only one purpose:

Temporary spatial synchronization between Orion and Aster.

Power Source
Echo Relay is powered by Echoes.

However, due to Orion's efficient engineering, the device consumes only a very small amount of Echoes for each activation.

The low cost reflects its role as a utility device rather than a combat ability.

Primary Functions
Orion → Aster
Allows Orion to instantly travel to Aster whenever his engineering expertise is required.

Common uses include:

Weapon repair

Echo transfer

Ancient machinery interaction

Puzzle solving

Story events

Aster → Orion
Allows Orion to summon Aster whenever hostile enemies or dangerous conditions prevent him from safely completing engineering tasks.

After Aster clears the threat, Orion proceeds with the required interaction.

Return Relay
Echo Relay stores both characters' previous positions before teleportation.

After completing the required objective, Orion may return both characters to their original locations, allowing exploration to continue without unnecessary travel.

Gameplay Restrictions
Echo Relay cannot be activated while Aster is actively engaged in combat.

The system is designed to support exploration, engineering, and cooperative gameplay rather than serving as a combat escape mechanic.

Design Philosophy
Echo Relay exists to reduce repetitive backtracking while strengthening the cooperative identity of the game.

Rather than replacing exploration, it allows players to focus on meaningful gameplay such as combat, engineering, puzzle solving, and resource management.
