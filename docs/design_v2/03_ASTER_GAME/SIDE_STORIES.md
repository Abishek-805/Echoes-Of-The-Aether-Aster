# Side stories, collectibles and completion

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

| Quest | Start / evidence | Completion | Reward / narrative |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Last Survey | Riven TOW03; DAWN logs in HUN/CAM/SLU/REL/SUR | All 8 DAWN records | Explain spatial failure; archive completion |
| A Keeper's Promise | SIL04 | Match recorded name to imprint (M12) | MEM03 and Seren lore |
| The Forgotten Garden | ROT03 | M06 after Sluice | HP01 and shortcut |
| A Hunter's Return | HUN04/CAM04 | Recover tag and read report | Materials; record in journal rather than return to inaccessible town |
| The First Warning | SIL04/FOL01/FOL03 | Read AR16, AR20, AR21 | Custodian/Continuum warning; no full reveal |

| The Engineer's Trace | VER02/VER04 | Read AR12 and AR13 | Orion connection; no usable repair part in I |

Completion is optional. Side stories do not block main route and are resolved via journal entries at site; no NPC turn-in after Warp. Town records automatically retained. Side rewards not sold or consumed as quest flags. Collectibles totals: 24 archive records, 8 Dawn logs, 6 map scraps, 4 +10 HP upgrades, 6 blueprints, 3 optional weapons, 5 memorials. Each has unique ID and room placement in [room specifications](ROOM_SPECIFICATIONS.md). Critical record entries may count toward archive total while being automatically recorded; other entries require inspection. 100% completion measures unique IDs and optional bosses, not repeat kills.

## Reward rules
Pickups behind challenge are committed only after challenge success; e.g. FOL04 HP04/MAP06/MEM05 require Devourer victory; ROT04 MAP04 requires Titan victory. Optional MAP scraps reveal annotations, not mandatory gates. No lootbox, random drop requirement or hidden ending requirement. Completion menu shows missed accessible region category without exact coordinates until player explores.
