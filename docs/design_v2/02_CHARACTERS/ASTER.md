# Aster — character bible

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

Male, 24–27, experienced Gold-ranked hunter from Eldara. Orphaned young, raised by Mira in a guild-town orphanage; admired hunters who protected the community. Not a chosen descendant, soldier, mercenary or superhero. Confident, protective, curious, impulsive, reliable, mobile. He seeks achievement and the promise of avoiding future mistakes, not resurrecting parents. Fears being powerless to protect others; believes reliance on others is weakness. I: wins his hunt and survival, accepts limits, asks for help. II: learns mutual trust. III: protects while accepting irreversible loss.

## Visual lock
Practical short slightly messy hair, calm observant eyes, human face, no ornamental armor, no capes/tattoos/implants. Six layers: body, expedition suit, jacket, harness, light armor, external equipment. Reinforced shoulders/knees, composite guards, fitted jacket, mobile boots, compact pack, utility belt. Brushed alloy, matte expedition fabric, polymer grips, visible bolts/hinges/straps. Maintain silhouettes: rifle, shoulders, backpack, forearm. Before acquisition right forearm mount is ordinary and inert; after relic installation cyan channels briefly pulse. No clock motif. Older art source retains compatible turnarounds, fabric/material and animation notes in archive; old reload and initial-Rewind assumptions are invalid.
