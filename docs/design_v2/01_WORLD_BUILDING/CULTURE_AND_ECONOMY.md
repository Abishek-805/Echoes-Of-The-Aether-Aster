# Culture, economy and everyday life

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

Eldaran hunters supply food, materials, safety and relics. Guild Marks pay for ordinary goods before the Warp; guild prices and rank describe social structure, not a full simulation. Relic Commission restricts unsafe artifacts; salvage agreements reward maps and safety reports. Families attend Release Houses to mark a death, using controlled release without destruction. Echo Energy can power technology but an Echo itself is not legal tender. Verayan researchers share instruments and peer-review findings; some see hunter expeditions as risky, while hunters value practical experience. Ancient Vaelari memorial culture is the origin of the trilogy's ethical tension.

Town commerce is short and meaningful: Aster begins with 60 Marks; optional bandage pouch 30, alloy pack 20, map annotation 10. Default kit is free. Purchase choices cannot block progression and Marks are not needed after Warp. Workbenches use salvaged materials. No live merchants deep inside the dungeon. Aster's refuge contains service station, recycled containers and a safe water supply; survival resources are narrative, not hunger meters.
