# Character Canon

## Aster

- Aster is male.
- In his first game, Aster is an experienced, talented hunter from Eldara.
- His parents died when he was a child. He grew up in an orphanage and was inspired by
  the hunters around him to train and become a hunter.
- He is skilled with combat and varied equipment, including guns and other gear.
- He is the player-controlled protagonist of *Echoes of the Aether: Aster*.
- Echo Rewind is his only special ability. He does not begin with it; he finds it
  around the middle of his game.
- The existing character source depicts him as practical, mobile, confident, curious
  and protective, and describes Echo Rewind as affecting Aster's own recent position
  and personal state, not the world, enemies or Orion. Compatible details from that
  source remain part of his character reference.
- At the end of his first game, he is trapped at the final gate because the Echo Warp
  has broken it beyond his ability to open.

## Orion

- Orion is from Veraya and is the player-controlled protagonist of
  *Echoes of the Aether: Orion*.
- He is an exceptionally talented engineer and strategist, highly intelligent and
  quick to understand unfamiliar technology.
- In his solo game, Orion enters the merged-dungeon crisis and helps Aster escape by
  solving engineering problems and the final gate.
- Aster accompanies Orion as an NPC ally who fights beside him. The player does not
  control Aster in this game.
- Orion's ability is Echo Intel, which helps him analyze devices, understand their
  workings and determine how they can be repaired. It does not perform repairs by
  itself.
- Orion has an unfinished concept for Echo Relay, but its advanced space-time
  teleportation is beyond what he can complete during his solo game.
- Orion and Aster become close friends through helping each other escape. They begin
  their joint journey at the end of Orion's game.

## Relationship across the trilogy

- **Aster's game:** Aster is alone and learns to survive the dungeon crisis.
- **Orion's game:** Orion is controlled by the player; Aster supports him as an NPC
  ally. Their friendship develops through shared danger and mutual help.
- **The Lost Echoes:** Both characters appear together in the planned co-op finale.
