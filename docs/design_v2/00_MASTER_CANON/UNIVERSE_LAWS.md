# Universe laws and constraints

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

Matter is physical existence; Echo is a dead person's remaining impression/unspoken voice; Aether is their destination beyond observable physical existence. The following laws govern all titles.

1. Origin: death of a person produces an Echo; technology cannot manufacture a genuine Echo or resurrect the person.
2. Passage: unobstructed Echoes naturally pass to Aether. It cannot be visited with a ship or gate. Whether consciousness persists beyond death is unknown.
3. Resonance: certain matter and devices can temporarily bind or route Echoes; recorded imprints are not living persons.
4. Release: safely releasing a bound Echo lets it continue; the release liberates recoverable energy. Only that energy is stored and spent by equipment.
5. Destruction: Voruun directly burn Echoes; destroyed Echoes are unrecoverable. Defeating a consumer cannot recover already destroyed Echoes.
6. Distortion: extreme Echo concentration destabilizes local space-time. Exact timing/location unpredictable with current modern technology; ancient network nodes are especially susceptible. Local flow control reduces risk but cannot shut down the global source.

## Warp boundaries
Localized folds connect regions across distance; planets do not fuse. Folds can last moments or days or longer, displace creatures, alter connections and damage hardware. Ordinary pathways remain deterministic after authored story changes. Space-time guardians exploit limited existing folds, not full Warp generation. Warp is not Echo Relay. Transit gates are ancient fixed infrastructure, not free teleportation. Rewind changes only Aster's recent validated position and selected personal state. No external machinery, Echo Energy, weapon heat, enemies, loot or death can be reversed.

## Technology tiers
Modern safe release has modest efficiency; ancient resonant circuits are more efficient (learned in III). Ancient technology is physical, constrained by hardware, energy and valid spatial anchors. Intel is diagnosis, not instant repair. Relay is controlled local spatial synchronization between validated endpoints only after its quest in III. No ability bypasses authored mandatory progression without explicit support.
