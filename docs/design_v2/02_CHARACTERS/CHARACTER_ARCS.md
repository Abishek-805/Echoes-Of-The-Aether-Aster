# Character arcs and thematic continuity

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

| Beat | Aster | Orion |
|---|---|---|
| Before crisis | Skilled and independent, admires hunter achievement | Confident in diagnosis and careful evidence |
| I opening | Chooses solo survey of familiar grounds | Off-screen researcher |
| I midpoint | Relic found; tempted to believe mistakes can always be reversed | Anonymous research evidence |
| I ending | Defeats guardian but cannot repair gate; asks for aid | Receives emergency transmission |
| II | Accepts engineer's guidance, fights beside him | Acts without full information, accepts fieldcraft |
| III | Protects others, relinquishes power over loss | Balances invention with responsibility |
| End | Partnership, no resurrection of parents | Safe release, not unlimited power |

Rules: no destiny bloodline, no secret parent twist, no instantaneous friendship or romance requirement. Aster's loss is revealed gradually (Mira, memorial, silent gallery). Orion sometimes misinterprets a machine; Aster's field observations correct him. Aster's bravery sometimes creates a problem Orion must solve. Their motifs combine in III. The Great Fracture embodies the same error on a civilization scale: believing nothing should be lost. Ending resolves their emotional development through action rather than exposition.
