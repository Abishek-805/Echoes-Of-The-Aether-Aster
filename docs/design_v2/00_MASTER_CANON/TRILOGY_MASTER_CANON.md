**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

## Authority
This package records the creator's acceptance of the expanded V2 design, with two explicit corrections: all ranged weapons are heat-based (no reload/ammunition/magazines), and the carried Echo Collector releases collected/interacted-with Echoes and stores **Echo Energy**, not the departed themselves. Latest direct creator decisions override this package. Narrative names and plot direction are approved; numerical values, new room-level details, scripts and engineering choices below are prototype specifications, not externally tested facts. See [decision register](CANON_DECISIONS.md) and [source audit](SOURCE_REGISTER.md).

## Identity
*Echoes of the Aether* is a spacefaring science-fantasy trilogy, 2D pixel art, Godot. Its emotional conflict is preservation versus acceptance; strength, knowledge and responsibility complement one another. The world is one connected puzzle, not disconnected levels. Combat is challenging but readable. No chosen bloodline or resurrection; Aether is not a planet.

| Installment | Protagonist | Arc | Ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| Echoes of the Aether: Aster | Aster, solo hunter | Seek Rewind, survive merged dungeon, learn limits of strength | Defeats Fold Guardian, finds physically broken gate, creates refuge and sends distress signal; cannot escape |
| Echoes of the Aether: Orion | Orion, solo engineer; Aster NPC after meeting | Investigate transmission, acquire Intel, cooperate to repair gate | Both escape to Veraya, combine clues and decide to seek Nalora |
| Echoes of the Aether: The Lost Echoes | Aster and Orion, two-player co-op | Survive Nalora, understand Great Fracture, complete Relay, restore ship and free remaining trapped Echoes | Controlled release, central collector shut down, ship leaves; one canonical ending with optional epilogue differences |

## Immutable continuity until revised
Eldara and Veraya are distinct planets. Their sanctuary pathways, not their planets or dimensions, are joined by uncontrolled Echo Warp. Nalora lies at the edge of the known universe, home of ancient Vaelari, rumored by modern peoples. The Voruun were displaced from an unnamed world; they destructively consume Echoes. Vaelari relics were shared as keepsakes, memorials and tools in protected Resonance Sanctuaries. Their failed Convergence Project caused the Great Fracture and continuing distortions. No protagonist deliberately creates or reverses Warp. Aster gains only Echo Rewind at Chapter 2 end; Orion gains Echo Intel in his own game, which diagnoses but never repairs automatically. Echo Relay is unfinished until Game III and never a separate Aster ability. Echoes are not ammunition, currency, or ordinary fuel; devices release them safely and use stored energy.

## Core design targets
Game I: 8–10 hours including optional exploration, 3 chapters, 6 shared biome art families, 16 regions, 60–80 rooms, 8 enemy archetypes + 4 variants, 4 required bosses and 2 optional bosses. Game II: shorter 3–4 hour, one/two chapter rescue. Game III: six chapters, final crisis resolved. Only Game I is production priority; later game designs are continuity outlines. No multiplayer requirement in I or II.
