# Game II — Echoes of the Aether: Orion

**Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-27 | **Status:** Approved V2 direction; implementation details are working specifications

Approved story direction; later detailed game design not required for Game I release. Solo 3–4h, one/two chapters, Veraya. Theme: knowledge must be applied responsibly. New geography and puzzles, not a duplicate Aster run.

1. Orion researches relics, detects unusual node connection, receives Aster's diagnostic distress packet. Enters Verayan sanctuary with tools and heat-based sidearm; secondary localized Warp closes his surface route.
2. Acquires Echo Intel in research archive. Device contact, 1–2s analysis, purpose/fault/repair requirements; EE from his own Collector. Repair remains manual. He avoids threats with movement, tools, distractions and limited self-defense. Incomplete Relay hypothesis only.
3. Reaches Aster's refuge through trapped maintenance link, not a surface exit. Aster is an NPC ally; player controls Orion. Aster fights, Orion diagnoses, each corrects other's assumptions. Commands follow/hold/engage/retreat; safe recovery and catch-up, no escort babysitting.
4. Gate control core/local anchor operational, remote-anchor interface fractured. Orion identifies compatible replacement in Verayan annex inaccessible from Aster's side in I. Aster helps recover it from danger; Orion installs, aligns to existing remote endpoint and confirms safe release path. Fixed ancient gate repair, not Relay. They escape to Verayan surface.
5. Compare Rewind and Intel manufacture, match fragmented navigation references and Nalora clue, agree to joint expedition. Friendship grows through mutual help. Aster has no new special ability, Orion cannot create Warp or complete Relay yet.

Combat/heat/EE rules carry forward; Orion's strengths diagnosis and tools, not rifle mastery. Legacy smoke emitter may be used as ordinary cooldown utility after testing. No co-op or Aster control. Detailed levels, encounter data and full script are later production tasks. [Orion character](../02_CHARACTERS/ORION.md), [canon](../00_MASTER_CANON/TRILOGY_MASTER_CANON.md).
